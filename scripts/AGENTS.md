# Partner corpus rules

- The Filed Under pipeline's IR document store (~162k documents) is read through the partner data service: `ir/{T}.json` (index), `irstore/<object path>` (returns a 10-minute signed URL for an original or its extracted text) and `doctables/{T}/…` (Docling tables). Helpers in `src/lib/ir/store.ts`; the IR library merges store rows not already in `ir_documents` (by source URL), and the assistant reads them with `list_ir_documents`/`read_ir_document`. Heavy parsing stays server-side; the browser only renders. A store document with page text opens in the source panel: `getIrHtml` answers an `x:<id>` with a store reference, the browser reads the text through `irstore/` and `renditionHtml` lays it out like the app's own renditions (`table[data-page]` with a row per printed line, `table[data-sheet]`). Partner-corpus values carry the cited line and its page or worksheet (`quote`, `page`, `sheet` on `SourceRef`); `src/lib/source/locate.ts` finds that line on that page, then the printed figure in it.
- `scripts/partner-mirror.ts` copies the partner's universe, company and price files into the `datasets` bucket (the existing `getDataset` fallback), so a partner outage never blanks pages; rerun it after each partner refresh. The partner service lives at `filedunderai.vercel.app` because `www.filedunder.ai` is now this app's own domain.
- `scripts/partner-mirror-ir.ts` copies the partner IR store into `ir-documents/partner/<object path>` (originals + extracted text) and `datasets` (`ir/{T}.json`, `doctables/…`), tracked in the service-role-only `partner_mirror` table so reruns resume; `partnerFile` falls back to that copy when the partner service fails. `scripts/ir-import-partner.ts` registers mirrored originals as `ir_documents` rows (source `partner`, deduped by source URL/sha256) so our readers, renditions and series builds use them like any stored document.
- The assistant reads the pipeline's computed layer through `src/lib/research/intel.ts` (`get_kpis`, `get_kpi_signals`, `get_earnings_workup`, `get_earnings_season`, `get_quant_profile`, `search_doc_tables`, `get_call_analysis`, `get_call_themes`, `get_trial_catalysts`, `get_analyst_track_record`, plus the computed digest at the top of `get_company_dossier`). KPI files keep flows under `Q`/`A`, balances and ratios under `I` (point in time) and some trailing figures under `T`; read a series through `seriesOf`, never `values.Q` alone, or ratios like CET1 disappear. Workbook table-of-contents groups hold page numbers and are skipped.
