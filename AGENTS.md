# Filed Under — architecture rules

- Real data is generated by `bun scripts/ingest.ts` (SEC XBRL statements, filings, iXBRL segments, Form 4, 8-K releases, price + FRED snapshots); never hand-edit those JSON files — rerun the script so every number keeps its SEC accession source. Covered companies' `companies/*.json` and `prices/*.json` are NOT committed: the partner pipeline runs this ingest on a schedule and serves them (see the partner data service rule); scripts read them through `scripts/lib/datasets.ts`, tests use `src/test/fixtures/`. Only benchmark price files, macro, universe/manifest and derived files stay in `public/data/`.
- Statement building lives in pure modules under `src/lib/financials/` (shared by the ingest script, the app and tests); keep them free of Node/browser APIs.
- Total debt in the statement builder (`debtFromLines`, `totalDebtRow` in `build.ts`): current maturities are never counted twice (a long-term figure that includes them, by its concept or by the same amount tagged under one that does, gets only short-term borrowings added) and current notes are added beside plain `LongTermDebt` only when the filing's own total says so; convertible notes and drawn credit lines are read when no long-term debt concept is tagged. A tagged total replaces the lines only when there are none or they come to under three quarters of it; when the lines are under half of another debt amount tagged for the date, or convertible lines disagree with a tagged total, the total is left empty, and net debt and the debt ratios with it: a fragment is never shown as total debt. A current credit-line tag counts as a line, or is ignored, only when the current liabilities tagged for the date add up exactly with it, or without it (`currentLiabilityLine`); `PaymentsForCapitalImprovements` is capex only when the investing lines add up exactly with it and no other property outflow is tagged beside it (`capitalImprovementsProven`).
- Headline statement lines (`build.ts`, `schema.ts`): cash flow totals fall back to the continuing-operations subtotal only where no total is tagged; continuing-operations EPS and net income available to common are read only for periods where nothing (discontinued operations; preferred dividends, noncontrolling interests, participating securities) stands between them and the statement's line; a bank's contract revenue no larger than its noninterest income is never revenue; overlapping annual periods give one column (`dropOverlappingYears`). `scripts/ingest.ts` reads extension concepts only where a line lists them as its concepts, so a capital line spread over several extension components (NextEra, DTE) stays empty. A headline line that is empty for a large company is a defect to look for: build every company from SEC companyfacts and list the empties before and after a builder change.
- Statement currency is pure in `src/lib/financials/currency.ts`; the Financials tab reads up to four 10-Q/10-K filings the partner build lacks, newest first within a 25 MB budget, through `getFilingDocument` and appends their periods with `extend.ts` (served columns never rewritten, every value sourced to the filing, marked as read at view time). The Overview (`useFiledStatements`) and the assistant's `get_financials` (`fetchFiledStatements`) share that read: one plan (`readPlan`), one reader (`readFiledReports`), one query cache, all in `src/components/financials/currency.tsx`; the Earnings tab's Results sections read it too (`resultsModel`, `src/components/earnings/results-view.ts`: a quarter a 10-K adds is the year less the first nine months, marked "10-K · new"); other tabs use the scheduled build only. `scripts/financials-currency.ts` is the read-only EDGAR currency checker.
- The company Overview computes from those extended statements (selection pure in `src/lib/company/currency-view.ts`); panels that depend on them hold their pre-read height and the reader stays in place across the swap (`src/components/company/hold-height.tsx`), and `Quarter drivers` explains the newest quarter whenever its cards explain at least one figure, otherwise the served quarter's cards with the newer quarter named in the subtitle (`viewDrivers`, `src/lib/company/why.ts`). The company body stays invisible until Company notices settle, at most `NOTICE_WAIT_MS` (1.2 s, `src/components/company/header/notices.tsx`), so they land in one step. The Overview's Alt data section (`#ov-alt`) is `AltSnapshot`.
- Every displayed financial value must carry a `SourceRef` (accession + XBRL concept + period) or a `derived` formula whose parts are SourceRefs, so click-to-source works everywhere.
- Company and price datasets for covered tickers load first from the partner data service (`VITE_PARTNER_DATA_URL`, default `https://www.filedunder.ai/api/app-data`) with the user's Supabase access token; it serves the same `CompanyDataset`/`PriceSeries` files, rebuilt twice a week by the partner's scheduled run of `scripts/ingest.ts`, to signed-in users only. Server code (`src/lib/reports/inputs.server.ts`) reads the same service with the `PARTNER_DATA_KEY` secret in an `x-partner-key` header (never expose it to the browser). Bundled `public/data` and the `datasets` bucket are the fallback, so keep `getDataset` falling back on any error and keep the ingest output format stable (the partner runs this repo's ingest).
- Live prices (Yahoo) and macro (FRED) come from server functions in `src/lib/market.functions.ts`; the client falls back to `public/data` snapshots when the live call fails.
- SEC documents are fetched server-side (SEC blocks CORS and requires a User-Agent) through `src/lib/sec.functions.ts`, and rendered in a sandboxed `srcdoc` iframe (no scripts) so the parent can locate and highlight iXBRL facts.
- The signed-in product lives under `src/routes/_authenticated/` (`/app/...`); marketing and auth pages are public top-level routes.
- Public company pages (`/companies/$ticker`, snapshot pure in `src/lib/public-company.ts`) show only sourced annual lines; sector and industry come from the coverage list and a description the served profile lacks comes from the coverage details, so the page never disagrees with the app. Check these pages on live data after a change to names, formats or the statement builder: they are the product on real data for all covered companies.
- A negative money amount is written with a true minus before the currency mark ("−$27.18"): use `fmtPrice`, `fmtValueLong`, `fmtCompactUsd` or `fmtSigned` (`src/lib/format.ts`), and never write `$` straight before a signed number in markup or a template.
- AI features call the Lovable AI Gateway from server functions only, behind `requireSupabaseAuth`; shared AI outputs are cached in the service-role-only `ai_cache` table.
- tsconfig relaxes `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`, `exactOptionalPropertyTypes` and `noPropertyAccessFromIndexSignature` because the dataset code is index-heavy; keep `strict` on.
- Yahoo throttles shared IPs: every Yahoo call goes through `yahooGet` (alternate hosts, small concurrency pool); quotes and the price job fall back to Nasdaq (stocks/ETFs) and Cboe (VIX) in `src/lib/market-data.server.ts`, then to bundled closes, and news-dependent AI falls back to hosted web search — never let a 429 blank a page.
- Research-agent filing citations use `filing:TICKER:ACCESSION[:DOC]` links with an optional verbatim-phrase link title; the source panel searches that phrase to highlight the passage.
- The page never moves as a side effect of a control. The app shell's main region carries a scroll floor (`src/components/app/scroll-floor.ts`): it never gets shorter than where the reader is, so a tab, filter or drill-down that shows less does not pull the page up. A view that leaves before the next mounts (`AnimatePresence mode="wait"`) must hold its container's height across the swap (the IR Financial map does), or the browser's scroll anchoring follows the collapse. Measure with one fresh page per control: bring the control to the middle, press it, compare where it sits. A page never pins its own height from script: a min-height outlives the view it was measured on, and a grid taller than its rows spreads them down the page (the Financials tab once showed its view selector under an empty band). The few files that may set one are listed, with when each lets go, in `src/components/app/height-holds.test.ts`; a grid that can be held taller than its content packs its rows at the top (`content-start`).
- Layout grids that hold tables or charts must have an explicit column template (`grid-cols-1` at the base breakpoint) and panels carry `min-w-0`, otherwise wide content stretches the page on phones. A pinned first column inside a flat `Panel` is `bg-panel` (the panel's tint as an opaque colour, `src/styles.css`), not `bg-card`, which shows as a lighter column. Panels whose tables have a minimum width go side by side by a container query on the page's own root (`@container`, `@7xl:grid-cols-2`, as the Estimates tab does), not by a window breakpoint: with the sidebar open a 1440 px window leaves 1152 px, and two columns there cut the tables off.
- Web research goes through `src/lib/web.server.ts` (Tavily search via `TAVILY_API_KEY`, Firecrawl scrape via `FIRECRAWL_API_KEY`, auto-detecting direct vs gateway keys) and is exposed only through authenticated server functions in `src/lib/web.functions.ts`; web-sourced AI output must list its source URLs.
- Bank-only statement lines are stripped at ingest for companies whose industry isn't a bank/brokerage, so non-banks never show misleading 'net interest income'.
- Fresh data comes from managed scheduled jobs hitting `src/routes/api/public/jobs/$job.ts` (guarded by `authenticateCronRequest`); each run handles a small rotating slice (cursor in `job_state`) to stay within per-invocation limits, writes to Cloud tables, and `src/lib/data.ts` merges those rows over the bundled snapshots.
- Valuation history is point-in-time: each week's multiple uses only fundamentals already filed by that date (`src/lib/financials/valuation.ts`), so charts never look ahead.
- The Calls page and overview read the partner worker’s earnings indexes and archived speaker turns (issuer, event, and source checked). Verified stored web copies fill missing periods. Missing coverage and promise analyses are queued in job_state for the Render transcript worker, never scraped or model-generated on a page reload.
- Earnings tab figures are pure in `src/lib/earnings/quarters.ts` (growth, margins, release and surprise matching, pending release); call analytics in `src/lib/transcripts/insights.ts`; a third-party takeaway is shown only when every figure in it was said by management (`checkTakeaways`). A quarter's release is the last Item 2.02 8-K between the quarter end and its 10-Q/10-K (without that report, only a sole candidate), otherwise unmatched, never guessed; fourth-quarter EPS is the release's printed figure or, marked †, Q4 net income ÷ Q4 weighted diluted shares, used only when that rule reproduces reported EPS for the year and Q1 to Q3, else a dash.
- Statement line concepts may be prefix-qualified company extensions (e.g. `cop:...`); SEC companyfacts omits extensions, so ingest reads them from each parsed filing's XBRL and merges them into the facts before building statements.
- Investor-relations documents (SEC 8-K EX-99 exhibits, IR-site PDFs/XLSX/decks found via Firecrawl and Tavily) are stored permanently in the private `ir-documents` bucket with `ir_documents` metadata; PDFs/spreadsheets get an HTML table rendition (`scripts/ir-renditions.ts`) so every IR KPI fact carries a `{t,r,c}` cell locator into the stored copy.
- IR presentation: one system in `src/components/ir/typography.ts` (doc comment); every IR region is an `IrPanel` (`panel.tsx`) with a `data-section`; figures the documents contradict are held, never corrected (`src/lib/ir/suspect.ts`); the page unpacks and tidies its payload in `ir-page.worker.ts`. Details: `src/lib/ir/AGENTS.md`.
- IR read-time checks (`src/lib/ir/sec-check.ts`, `consistency.ts`) run in the page worker after the tidy: figures whose unit, sign or period the company's 10-Q/10-K contradicts are held, other differences are flagged at the figure with both numbers, guard-held figures the filing confirms are shown again; quarters are checked against the year and calculated values against their formula. Nothing is corrected, and the check is not a guarantee (it compares consolidated lines only). A figure that differs only from a later filing's comparative, by at most 15% (`RECAST_LIMIT`) and with no other line of the period agreeing, is status `later` (possibly recast), not counted as differing; rules in `src/lib/ir/AGENTS.md`. A 52/53-week quarter printed under two dates a week apart with identical figures is one column (`twins.ts`).
- Scheduled HTTP calls are cancelled after a few seconds: every job stops starting new work once `RUN_BUDGET_MS` (jobs.server.ts) is spent and resumes next run (`ir-docs` tracks done/partial/empty filings in the service-role-only `ir_release_scan`); deep history (`scripts/ir-backfill.ts`) and IR-site discovery (`scripts/ir-site.ts`) run as scripts.
- Rotating HTTP jobs move their cursor only after the slice is stored (`src/lib/jobs/slice.ts`, `<job>-slice` attempts, skip after 3); every unit gets a deadline from `RUN_BUDGET_MS` (3.5 s, `JOB_RUN_BUDGET_MS`), and they walk the top 1,000 by 20-day dollar volume first (`src/lib/jobs/priority.ts`).
- Provider failures are classed in `src/lib/jobs/failure.ts`: quota, payment and configuration errors close that provider's circuit (`provider:<id>` in `job_state`, `provider-health.ts`) and leave items untouched; one probe reopens it and paused lanes resume by themselves. Markers and weekly timers advance only after work was stored.
- The `emails` job runs the dead-man watchdog (`src/lib/jobs/watchdog.ts`): one deduplicated `ops-alert` digest per episode, at most every 30 min; `/app/freshness` shows providers, alerts, job health and top-1,000 coverage from `job_health()` and `data_coverage()` (migration 0066).
- A covered company that changed SEC registrant carries `formerCiks` (`src/lib/coverage.ts`): feed jobs read every CIK (`companyCiks`), statement history comes from `historyCik`, and an archive URL uses the CIK whose index lists the filing (`sec_filings.cik`), because SEC answers 404 under the other one. The worker's weekly `registrant-drift` check alerts when SEC's ticker file disagrees.
- Bulk pipeline scripts share the app's database: every unit of work waits on `dbGate()` (`scripts/lib/supa.ts`) and `runBatch` sheds workers when round-trips slow, so bulk reading never makes pages time out.
- Excel exports share `src/lib/export/xlsx-theme.ts` (palette, number formats, cover); cells hold full values and the $K/$M/$B scale lives in the number format, so formulas stay exact. The IR workbook (`src/lib/export/ir-xlsx.ts`) is built in a web worker because bank-sized exports take seconds to serialise.
- Supply-chain links come from `scripts/supply-chain.ts` reading each company's latest 10-K into `supply_links`; a link is stored only if its quote is found verbatim in the filing (`src/lib/supply/core.ts`), so every edge on the map opens its source sentence.
- The research agent loop lives in `src/lib/research/loop.ts` (server step ⇄ browser tools; owns plan/ask_user tools, retries, context compaction, approach restarts, deep-mode self-review) and powers the dockable/floating `AssistantPanel` (⌘J); browser tools run in `src/lib/research/execute.ts`, web map/crawl/extract/fetch go through authed `web.functions.ts`.
- Assistant on-page agency (scroll_to/highlight/read_section/click) is in `src/lib/research/dom-agent.ts`: it never matches inside the panel (`data-agent-ignore`) and clicks only tabs, toggles, segmented controls, `data-agent-click` elements and in-app links; give important page regions a `data-section="Name"` so the agent can target them. Canvas views/files/browser runs live in the in-memory store `src/lib/research/views.ts` (zod-validated view specs); files are built client-side in `files.ts`.
- The assistant's page knowledge (`src/lib/research/site-map.ts`, `trading-text.ts`, tool descriptions in `tool-defs.ts`) and the tour (`src/lib/onboarding/steps.ts`) are updated whenever a page's sections, controls, search params or claims change; `site-map.test.ts` pins the current section names.
- Cloud browsers use Browser Use v4 runs through `src/lib/browser-use.server.ts` with the `BROWSER_USE_API_KEY` secret, exposed only via authed `src/lib/browser.functions.ts`; every run handle carries an HMAC of (run id, user id), so users can only read or stop their own runs. Never send the key to the client. Run rules: `src/lib/browser/limits.ts`.
- Supply-chain exposure, second-order links and shock propagation are pure functions in `src/lib/supply/core.ts`; dollar exposure only comes from stated amounts or stated revenue shares × SEC revenue.
- Region names are classed in `src/lib/supply/place-kind.ts` (U.S. states and territories count as the United States, supranational regions are never countries, Georgia is decided from the sentence): a name is a country only when it is in that file's country table, cities and provinces count toward their country, and residual names ("Outside the United States", "Rest of world") are never a place; the stats strip's dependency cells, their directions and their words are in `src/lib/supply/strip.ts`, which uses the Dependency score's own count and label so the two never disagree.
- Technicals (`/app/markets`): everything shown is computed at read time by pure modules in `src/lib/technicals/` (`stat.ts`, `conditions.ts`, `evidence.ts`, `measures.ts`, `read.ts`, `pooled.ts`; entry `analysis.ts`, run in `analysis.worker.ts` through `client.ts`). A condition is called reliable only by `evidence.ts` (Hodrick standard errors for overlapping windows, Benjamini-Hochberg across everything tested for the symbol, bootstrap interval, cost, stability, cross-company support); every sentence of the read is assembled from figures it carries (`read.ts`), never written by a model. `ta.js` holds the indicator and event definitions shared with the pipeline: do not change them. New measures need a unit test against a hand or textbook value. The assistant's `get_quant_profile` quotes the same analysis (`technicalsFor` in `src/lib/research/intel.ts` assembles the page's input and runs `analyze()`); the pipeline's model-written quant read is shown nowhere, the dossier digest included.
- Sector pages: `/app/sectors`, `/app/sectors/$sector` and `/app/sectors/g/$slug` read the partner's `sectors.json`/`sectors/{slug}.json` (etl/sectors.py) plus the member tape from `setup_series` through `getSectorBreadth` (`src/lib/sectors/breadth.functions.ts`); medians, breadth, ranks and distributions are pure in `src/lib/sectors/` (tests beside them); view state lives in validated search params.
- Earnings reactions, chart patterns and correlations: pure in `src/lib/markets/core.ts`, built by `scripts/markets.ts`; worker-published analytics (markets/returns, auctions, hedge funds, context) live in `datasets/analytics/`, read via `src/lib/analytics-files.ts` with `public/data` as fallback.
- Supply-chain analyst metrics are pure in `src/lib/supply/core.ts`; the analyst note may only cite stored link ids.
- The end-to-end value chain (`src/lib/supply/chain.ts`) joins quoted supply links with XBRL-tagged customer/supplier shares (`xbrl_concentration`, names matched by `src/lib/xbrl/counterparty.ts`; rerun `scripts/xbrl-rematch.ts` after changing the matcher), so every edge keeps the filing that states it.
- `scripts/supply-intel.ts` fills `supply_signals` with verbatim-verified quotes; failed stages keep stored rows.
- Supply-chain views: Network (`supply-network.tsx`, layout pure in `src/lib/supply/network-layout.ts`) and Globe (`supply-globe.tsx` on the canvas engine in `components/supply/globe/`, placement pure in `src/lib/supply/geo.ts`) read the same `supply_links` graph as Chain; both open the filing sentence through `onSelect`. Head offices come from `bun scripts/company-hq.ts` into `public/data/hq.json` and `datasets/analytics/hq.json`: rows hand-checked against filing cover pages (`scripts/data/hq-verified.json`, sources per row) first, then the SEC business address from SEC's submissions index for every covered CIK (registered-agent addresses are never placed; pure rules in `src/lib/sec/address.ts`), then the pipeline directory only where SEC gives no usable city; the script refuses to publish a partial SEC read; each row records which, and the globe shows it on hover. A counterparty outside coverage is placed only by a strict whole-name home-country table or by the one country its quoted sentence names; otherwise it is listed as not placed, never guessed.
- `supply_links` is read only through `src/lib/supply/read.ts`, which verifies each row's amount, single-source flag and regions against its own quote (`trustLink`, claims.ts: an amount only as the counterparty's stated figure, flows only for revenue, purchases or contract value on trade links); `read.test.ts` fails on any other reader. Stored rows keep what the model returned; `scripts/supply-revalidate.ts` only reports. `dedupeCurrent` keeps the newest 10-K/10-Q row and carries figures only the older copy states, with their source (`figure_from`).
- Supply relation encoding (colour, shape, line style) lives in `components/supply/supply-map-meta.ts`; card and hover-card facts are pure in `src/lib/supply/describe.ts`; `/app/supply-chain` keeps `view`, `sel`, `overlay`, `depth`, `tab` and `iso` (what is isolated) in validated search params (`src/lib/supply/url-state.ts`) and never scrolls as a side effect of a control (`src/lib/supply/page-nav.ts`; rules in `src/lib/supply/AGENTS.md`); the globe's 1:10m map is packed in a worker (`globe/hi-geo.worker.ts`), which also draws the map for every zoom (`paint.ts`); its opening camera and wheel zoom rules are in `components/supply/globe/AGENTS.md`.
- Alt data (registry, gov files, leaderboards): see `src/lib/alt/AGENTS.md`.
- ABS-EE asset-level data (SEC Form ABS-EE, exhibit EX-102: commercial mortgage loans record by record, auto loan and lease files as pool figures) is stored by `scripts/abs-ee.ts` and the `abs-ee` job into the `abs_*` tables of migration 0067, as filed, with the accession, cover period and file hash of every filing, revisions kept and one filing stored all or nothing. The script gives back nothing to an SEC outage, and the worker stays on a CIK while it has filings left: see `src/lib/abs/AGENTS.md`.
- Alt figures and unusual-change rules are pure in `src/lib/alt/derive.ts`, `company.ts`, `hub.ts` and `history.ts`: counts are tested exactly against earlier whole windows that start after the first stored record and end where the source has fully published (`lagDays`; the month in progress is left out), readings against the company's own range; regulatory, contracts and trials are not watched, and every figure keeps its rows.
- OFR hedge fund data comes from `bun scripts/hedge-funds.ts` into `public/data/hedge-funds.json`, values as published with source URLs; rerun, never hand-edit.
- Hedge analytics: `src/lib/hedge/core.ts`.
- AI briefs/notes cite only browser-built fact ids.
- Promises keep only verbatim-verified quotes (`vetPromises`); alerts are pure diffs in `src/lib/alerts/core.ts` over sourced rows.
- Analyst estimates: `scripts/estimates.ts` fills `analyst_estimates` (provider throttles Node fetch); `getEstimates` refreshes live after 6h.
- Company context (Wikipedia description, Commons photos, 10-K/proxy people with headshots) comes from `bun scripts/company-context.ts` into `public/data/context/`; people are kept only if named in the filing text, and photos need a Wikidata link to the company or the company's own page.
- Firm workspaces: permissions live in the database (RLS, checked RPCs, guard triggers); firm tables `REVOKE ALL` then grant exact columns, because this project's default privileges grant everything.
- Report library: facts are built purely in `src/lib/reports/` and every model sentence is re-checked against them (`vet.ts`, sign-aware); slots are written only through `ensureReport` (`store.server.ts`, single flight + `report_runs` spend limits), shared by the authed server fn and `scripts/reports-pregen.ts`. Report charts and peer comparisons are built purely in `insights.ts`/`peers.ts`; every plotted value points at a fact (peer facts carry `tk`, medians/ranks are `composite` facts listing their members); bump `REPORT_FORMAT` when report data changes so stored reports are rewritten.
- Filing redlines are pure in `src/lib/redline/` (text blocks → sections → paragraph pairing → word diff → rule-based score); `scripts/redlines.ts` stores standard pairs in `filing_redlines`, other pairs are built and stored on the server by `computeRedline` (`src/lib/redline.functions.ts`), and briefs keep only bullets whose quote is verbatim in a cited change.
- Filing lists are pure in `src/lib/filings/` (`kinds.ts` form families and 8-K items in words, `list.ts` company list scope/paging and Form 4 rows from insider trades, `daily.ts` the day-by-day `sec_filings` feed, `redline-verdict.ts` the Filing changes verdict); pages keep their scope in validated search params.
- Partner-driven jobs: `src/routes/api/public/partner/$task.ts` (`status`, `report`, `supply`) is called by the Filed Under pipeline with `x-partner-key` = `PARTNER_DATA_KEY`; it writes shared standard reports through `ensureReport` (same spend limits) and supply links through `src/lib/supply/extract.server.ts` (shared with `scripts/supply-chain.ts`). Keep the request/response shapes stable.
- Access: sidebar hubs and their page tabs come from `src/lib/nav.ts`; `/app` checks `has_app_access` (waitlist approved or admin) and admin paths need `has_role(admin)`, enforced in RLS (research tables/`datasets` read via `has_app_access`) and `requireAppAccess` on research/AI server fns, because hiding a link isn't security.

- Logos: a file in `public/logos` stands for the covered company it is named after; check a mark against the company itself, preferably its own SEC filing, never against another derived file, and record where each file came from in `public/logos/sources.json` (Commons file, partner file, or the filing's form, date, accession and image). A ticker with no checked mark shows initials. `CompanyLogo` takes every size from `src/lib/logos.generated.ts` (`bun scripts/logo-manifest.ts`): a wide wordmark (ink at least 2.6:1, `LOGO_WIDE`) widens lg and xl plates and is not drawn in small tiles, which show the company's own site icon from `public/logos/icons` (recorded under `icon` in its `sources.json` entry) or the letters. After adding or replacing a file, remeasure it (`python3 -I scripts/logo-measure.py public/logos public/logos/aspect.json`) before the manifest; a file whose bytes no longer match its measurement fails the test. A name without a ticker is drawn under `logoKey` (`src/lib/supply/logo-key.ts`), never under the first letters of its name, so it cannot borrow a covered company's mark.
- Small tiles of a wide wordmark with no square icon of its own draw a monogram (`LOGO_MONOGRAMS`: letters of the name on the wordmark's measured ink colour, contrast rule in `scripts/logo-manifest.ts`, by-eye overrides in `public/logos/monogram-neutral.json`); a monogram is not a logo and `sources.json` marks each one. Marks are measured by `scripts/logo-measure.py` into `public/logos/aspect.json`.
- Coverage names: `scripts/expand-coverage.ts` takes a display name or Wikidata item only when it names the SEC registrant behind the CIK (`scripts/lib/coverage-names.ts`), and shows it through `displayName` (SEC's state and successor markers removed, trailing legal forms cut, "& Co." and initials kept; a casing the rule cannot know comes from a correction); SEC-sourced fixes live in `scripts/data/coverage-corrections.json` and apply only while the old value still matches (`applyCorrections`); `--from-existing` reapplies them without the partner universe.
- Coverage details (website, IR address, founding year, description of the generated list) live in the server-only `src/lib/coverage-details.server.ts`; `scripts/ingest.ts` reads them into each profile. Values SEC data or the company's own 8-K contradicts are fixed by sourced entries in `scripts/data/coverage-details-corrections.json` (pure step in `scripts/lib/coverage-details.ts`, applied by `expand-coverage.ts` in both modes): a description of another entity becomes the SEC-built fallback sentence, a website no filing gives is removed. SEC's industry text is shown through `sicDescription` (`src/lib/sec/sic.ts`), which replaces the few codes whose submissions text disagrees with SEC's own code list (1220, 5051); the page, the ingest and the fallback sentence all use it. A wrong IR address sends the IR-site collector to another company; files already stored from one are refused by `src/lib/ir/source-guard.ts` (see `src/lib/ir/AGENTS.md`). IR-site search hits on content networks (q4cdn, gcs-web) are taken only on evidence (`src/lib/jobs/ir-site-match.ts`): the company's own host on a label boundary, the network folder its stored files already use, its distinctive full name or its ticker in capitals as a whole word; never a substring.
