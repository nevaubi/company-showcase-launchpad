# New features before launch

Three features that give users a reason to come back daily, each built on data we already have and every line source-linked.

1. **Watchlist alerts** — follow companies and get an in-app inbox (bell icon) when something changes: new filing, insider buy/sell, new or dropped supply-chain link, alt-data event, trade idea firing, or an IR number that moved sharply. Each alert opens its source.
2. **Earnings preview and recap** — on each company page: before earnings, a sourced setup (what to watch, KPI trends, past reactions, open promises); after the call, a "what changed" recap comparing new numbers and statements against last quarter.
3. **Management promise tracker** — pulls forward-looking commitments (guidance, launch dates, margin targets, buybacks) from calls and filings, then marks each Kept / Missed / Pending against later reported numbers, with both quotes linked. Shows a company "credibility score".

Then a signed-in check of all three on NVIDIA and Apple, desktop and phone.

## Technical details
- Tables: `watch_alerts` (per-user, RLS), `promises` (shared, read-only to users). Alerts generated by a new rotating slice in the existing scheduled jobs route; promises extracted by a bounded `scripts/promises.ts` from stored transcripts and 8-K releases, quote-verified verbatim like supply signals.
- Promise grading and alert diffing as pure tested modules under `src/lib/`.
- Preview/recap via an authenticated server function on the AI gateway, citing only assembled fact ids (same pattern as the morning brief), cached in `ai_cache`.
- New company tab "Earnings" and header bell; assistant gets `get_promises` and `get_alerts` tools.
