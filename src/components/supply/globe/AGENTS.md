# Supply-chain globe engine
- No React and no app imports in this folder. `engine.ts` (browser-only) draws; every rule it draws by is pure and tested beside it: `sphere.ts` (projection, zoom that holds the pointed-at place, framing, flights), `geometry.ts` (packed maps, level of detail, tracing), `cluster.ts` (badge sizes, clusters, fans, rim groups), `arcs.ts` (link paths, bundles, widths), `labels.ts` (name placement). `atlas.ts` holds logos at exact device-pixel sizes.
- Two canvases: the map (base) is redrawn only when the camera, theme, tints or map data change; links, badges and names (top) repaint on hover. The frame loop stops when nothing moves.
- Level of detail depends on scale only, never on motion or on where the camera points: each vertex carries an importance (Douglas-Peucker per topology arc, so neighbours drop the same border points) and a frame keeps what matters at its scale. 1:110m is bundled for the first frame; 1:50m and 1:10m are packed in `hi-geo.worker.ts` and transferred.
- Shores and borders are stroked exactly one device pixel wide (`0.999 / dpr`): that is the hairline the browser draws fastest; a wider stroke with round joins cost six times the rest of the frame.
- Wheel and touch: a plain wheel scrolls the page until the map has been pressed, zooms toward the pointer after that (never passing a zoom limit on to the page) and belongs to the page again once the pointer leaves; Ctrl or ⌘ + wheel and two fingers always zoom; one finger pans the page until a tap has engaged the map. Every `focus()` passes `preventScroll`.
- Flat map is Miller's cylindrical projection: it repeats sideways and pans like a street map, so nothing re-projects while it moves.
- Links are bundled per pair of markers and style (`bundleArcs`); width is the host's value on the Network view's square-root scale (`flowWidth`). More than a dozen links leaving the frame are cut to stubs.
- The canvas publishes its view at rest in `data-view="lon,lat,zoom,globe|flat"` for tests and the assistant.
- Opening and reset camera: `openingCamera` (`sphere.ts`): the `fitCamera` framing when everything fits on the near side; when the company's places spread over more than a hemisphere, the centre that shows the most weight with the company well inside the disc. One wheel notch is 1.25× (`wheelFactor`, at most 1.3× an event).
- During a wheel or pinch zoom the last traced map is moved and scaled by a CSS transform (`reuseBase` in `loop.ts`, within `RETRACE_RATIO` of the traced scale); the frame after the gesture always traces again, so the map is sharp at rest.
