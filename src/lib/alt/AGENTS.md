# Alt data rules

- `scripts/alt-data.ts` upserts `alt_data` (stable `uid`, `source_url` required) for every covered company, using the name registry built by `scripts/alt-registry.ts` (`datasets/alt/registry.json`; hand-checked maps in `collect.server.ts` win, names matching two filers are dropped); contract recipients match by name prefix; SEC form maps and signals are pure in `src/lib/alt/`. Whole-market gov files (SEC fails-to-deliver, CMS Medicare drug spending) are parsed purely in `src/lib/alt/ftd.ts`/`medicare.ts` and loaded by `scripts/gov-data.ts`; cross-company series leaderboards come from the `alt_series_leaders` database function, never from paging raw rows.
- Bank deposits by state come from the FDIC Summary of Deposits (`src/lib/alt/sod.ts`, pure; `bun scripts/gov-data.ts --sod`), rolled up over every bank under the company's holding company (`RSSDHCR`, else the bank's own certificate); a state row's market share divides by the FDIC's all-institution total for that state and year. The survey is yearly (June 30), so the IR loop runs `--sod --if-new` weekly and a full import only follows a newer year. FDIC certificates come from the registry; the fuzzy fallback (`pickFdicFuzzy`) accepts a bank only through its holding company's core name (its own name only when it has no holding company) and refuses a name shared by a similar-sized bank under another owner.
- Medicare drug costs by state come from CMS's yearly "Part D Prescribers – by Geography and Drug" files (`src/lib/alt/medicare-geo.ts`, pure; `bun scripts/gov-data.ts --medicare-states`); a brand counts for a company only when labelers resolving to it carry ≥95% of the brand's spending that year in the Part D Spending by Drug file, so multi-source generics and co-labeled brands are left out and the total row reports the covered share. Each year links its own CMS dataset version (never the moving "latest" alias); the IR loop runs `--medicare-states --if-new` weekly.
