# Signals, setups and autopilots

- Trade ideas come from fixed, point-in-time rules in `src/lib/signals/core.ts`, precomputed by `bun scripts/signals.ts` into `public/data/signals.json` with each rule's historical hit rate vs SPY; rerun after ingest. Paper trades live in the per-user `paper_trades` table.
- Short-term setups: strategies (`src/lib/signals/strategies.ts`), point-in-time price/event features (`features.ts`, `events.ts`: 8-K 2.02 earnings, Form 4 buys, FINRA short volume, SEC fails-to-deliver used only after its publication lag, VIX3M), the one-ticker scan (`scan.ts`), the out-of-sample gate (`backtest.ts`, per-day aggregates so DB and memory give identical stats) and autopilots (`bots.ts`) are pure and shared by `bun scripts/setups.ts` (full history, reseeds `setup_samples` and `datasets/setups/bench.json`) and the nightly `setups` job (`src/lib/jobs/setups.server.ts`: bars → context → scan → cross → evaluate → publish → bots, leased/claimed so parallel schedules are safe). The live record counts only signals with `first_seen = fired_at`. Setups are proven only when every gate check passes; never loosen the gate to fill the page.
- Event inputs are backfilled by `scripts/short-volume.ts` (FINRA), `scripts/insider-history.ts` + `scripts/insider-recent.ts` (Form 4 purchases) and `scripts/earnings-events.ts` (8-K 2.02); the `sec` job keeps `earnings_events`/`insider_buys` current, the setups job reads FINRA files itself and fails-to-deliver from `alt_data` (`gov-data` job); the script caches the SEC fails files since 2018. Rerun `bun scripts/setups.ts` after changing any strategy or feature so stored samples match the new rules.
- What the setups page shows is decided at read time, in pure modules: `evidence.ts` turns each variant's stored monthly curve into a month-by-month record since 2022 (error widened by Newey-West for holds that run across months), puts Benjamini-Hochberg q-values across every variant tried, shrinks averages toward zero, tests regime differences against noise and puts a rule on probation once six or more live holding periods sit well below its backtest; `rank.ts` builds the short list from listed rules only (proven, candidate at 5%, watch at 10%), one per ticker, five per rule and sector, a $20M a day floor by default, and never ranks a row whose stored bars are implausible; `explain.ts` words the verdict; `url-state.ts` keeps view and filters in the address. `setupsQuery` computes the evidence once per load and keeps it in the query cache. Never fill the short list with signals of unlisted rules: the "closest" view exists for that and says it is a watch list.
- Day aggregates are not independent when holds overlap: `stats.ts` has `hacSe`/`tHac` (Bartlett weights on the trading-day grid) and `backtest.ts` stores `tHac` and gates on `adjustedT` (the smaller of the two). Stored stats carry `tHac` only after `bun scripts/setups.ts` is rerun or the worker runs the new code; until then the gate reads the old `t`, and the page relies on the monthly record instead.
- `variants-real.fixture.ts` holds the 46 production variant records of 20 Nov 2026 (checksum-verified transfer) so tests pin the audit's numbers; replace it the same way, never by hand.
- Trade ideas are ranked in `ideas.ts`: a rule's history is read against a coin flip, and the short list keeps an idea only when one of its reasons comes from a rule that made money against the S&P 500 over 100 or more firings.
