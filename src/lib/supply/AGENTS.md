# Supply-chain rules
- Supply-chain country/region/concentration/freshness/basket analytics are pure in `src/lib/supply/analytics.ts`; web cross-checks keep only sentences naming both companies.
- Region risk: pure in `src/lib/supply/region.ts`; external lookups via authed `supply-region.functions.ts`, cached, sourced.
- Globe placement is pure in `src/lib/supply/geo.ts` (head office, then a known head-office country, then the single country the filing names; otherwise not placed); network geometry is pure in `network-layout.ts`. The canvas engine under `components/supply/globe/` takes no app imports.
- `/app/supply-chain` never moves the reader. Every address change it makes spreads `VIEW_NAV` or `COMPANY_NAV` (`page-nav.ts`, both `resetScroll: false`); the one scroll call is `components/supply/scroll-to.ts`, used only by the section links and the off-screen cue's button; `.focus()` always passes `preventScroll`. A click whose result is out of view raises the `OffscreenCue` (a slim fixed bar with a button), never a scroll. `page-nav.test.ts` scans the route and the supply components for anything else; `.preview/scroll_check.py` measures it in a browser.
- Isolation is one search param, `iso` (`rel:supplier`, `node:supplier:TSM`, `path:ENTG>TSM>NVDA>HPE`), read by `parseIsolation` and resolved by `resolveIsolation` (`isolate.ts`) into the lit map keys, the lit chain nodes and edges, a label and the pathway in order. Views take `IsolateProps` and raise `IsolateRequest`s; the route turns a request into the next state with `nextIsolation` (the same request twice clears; a supplier pick and a customer pick join into one pathway). Lists follow with `isolatedHas`. A pathway is entity keys (ticker, else `entityKey` of the name), upstream to downstream, the company in its place.
- Chain geometry is pure in `chain-layout.ts`. A chain link's stated share keeps what it is a share of (`ShareBasis` in `chain.ts`): a share of the seller's revenue or of the buyer's purchases always outranks a receivables share (often an older period) and a reseller's share of its own revenue, which is never labelled purchases.
- On this page no panel collapses (`Panel collapse={false}`), no list hides rows behind "show more" and nothing scrolls inside a box: the Network draws every name, long coverage-wide lists are capped with the count stated, the statement list is paged.
